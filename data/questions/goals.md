# goals.md
- What is one thing you’d like to learn, make, or accomplish this year—and why does it matter to you?
- If you made steady progress on one goal over the next month, what would you hope to notice?
- What small, specific step could you take this week toward something you want?
- What might get in the way of that step, and what could help you follow through?
- How would you know you were making progress, even if you hadn’t reached the finish line?
- What does success mean to you apart from getting a good grade or praise from someone else?
- Which goal feels like yours, and which might come from other people’s expectations?
- If your plans changed, what would you want to keep aiming for?
- What is something you tried before that you’d like to approach differently this time?
- Who could encourage or support you as you work toward a goal?
